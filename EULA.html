Last Updated: May 20, 2026

Introduction

Kolly is committed to building a safe, positive, respectful, and inspiring beauty community focused on eyelash design, lash inspiration, AI-assisted beauty conversations, and creator interaction.

Because Kolly includes user-generated content, AI conversation systems, creator uploads, profile systems, comments, messaging tools, and community interaction features, all users must comply with these Community Guidelines and EULA Supplement in addition to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

These rules apply to all activities occurring within the Kolly platform, including uploaded content, eyelash photos, comments, AI conversations, creator posts, profile information, messages, beauty references, and all other user interactions.

Kolly reserves the right to investigate violations of these rules and may take moderation or enforcement actions including content removal, warnings, reduced visibility, AI feature restrictions, temporary suspensions, permanent account bans, preservation of moderation evidence, or cooperation with law enforcement where required.

These guidelines may be updated periodically to reflect changes in platform features, legal obligations, safety requirements, AI systems, or App Store policies.

1. Compliance With Law

Users must comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws while using Kolly.

Any activity that promotes, facilitates, encourages, or solicits illegal conduct is prohibited.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • fraud or deceptive activity;
  • phishing or scams;
  • identity theft;
  • unauthorized access to accounts or systems;
  • distribution of malware or harmful software;
  • illegal cosmetic services;
  • unlawful sale or promotion of products;
  • copyright infringement;
  • illegal use of personal information.

Users may not use Kolly to organize or coordinate unlawful activities.

Kolly may cooperate with law enforcement, platform providers, regulators, payment providers, or other authorities when required by law or necessary to protect platform safety.

2. Violence, Threats, And Dangerous Conduct

Kolly has zero tolerance for threats of violence, intimidation, or dangerous conduct.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • threats of physical harm;
  • encouragement of violence;
  • violent intimidation;
  • promotion of terrorism or extremist activity;
  • graphic violent content;
  • instructions intended to facilitate serious harm;
  • threats involving weapons.

Users may not harass, stalk, intimidate, or threaten others through the Service.

Accounts associated with credible threats or violent conduct may be permanently suspended without prior notice.

3. Harassment, Hateful Conduct, And Bullying

Kolly does not allow harassment, hateful conduct, bullying, discrimination, or abusive behavior.

Users may not attack or degrade individuals or groups based on characteristics including:

  • race;
  • ethnicity;
  • nationality;
  • religion;
  • gender;
  • gender identity;
  • sexual orientation;
  • disability;
  • medical condition;
  • age;
  • immigration status;
  • veteran status.

Harassment includes:

  • repeated abusive comments;
  • stalking behavior;
  • coordinated harassment;
  • humiliation or intimidation;
  • degrading sexual comments;
  • malicious false reporting;
  • encouragement of abuse against other users.

Severe or repeated violations may result in permanent account removal.

4. Sexual Content And Nudity

Kolly is a beauty and eyelash inspiration platform.

Pornography, sexually explicit content, sexual exploitation, or adult solicitation are prohibited.

Users may not:

  • upload pornography;
  • promote sexual services;
  • distribute explicit sexual content;
  • exploit minors;
  • share non-consensual intimate content;
  • upload sexually exploitative material.

Beauty-related eyelash demonstrations and close-up facial content may be permitted when relevant to the Service and presented appropriately.

Kolly may remove content that is sexually suggestive, exploitative, unsafe, or inconsistent with the intended beauty purpose of the platform.

5. User Privacy And Personal Information

Users must respect the privacy of others.

You may not share another person’s private or identifying information without authorization.

This includes:

  • full names;
  • addresses;
  • phone numbers;
  • government identification;
  • payment information;
  • private messages;
  • private salon information;
  • private customer records;
  • location information;
  • confidential beauty or medical details.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information into AI conversations or public areas of the Service.

Kolly may remove content that violates reasonable privacy expectations.

6. Impersonation And Misrepresentation

Users may not impersonate another person, beauty creator, lash artist, salon, business, celebrity, or Kolly representative.

False claims of professional certification, endorsements, or official affiliations are prohibited.

Attempts to falsely represent yourself as a Kolly employee, moderator, or support representative may result in immediate suspension.

7. Ai Conversation And Ai System Rules

Kolly includes AI-assisted conversation and beauty recommendation features.

Users must use these systems responsibly.

Users may not use AI systems to:

  • generate illegal content;
  • create abusive or hateful material;
  • produce misleading medical advice;
  • manipulate platform systems;
  • generate spam;
  • bypass moderation systems;
  • harass or exploit other users;
  • generate unsafe cosmetic instructions.

Kolly’s AI models are internally managed and specifically designed for eyelash beauty and inspiration scenarios.

The AI systems are built and managed according to Kolly’s business requirements and safety standards. Kolly does not rely on third-party public AI chat platforms to directly provide user-facing AI conversation features.

AI-generated outputs are intended for informational and beauty inspiration purposes only.

Users remain responsible for evaluating AI responses before relying on them.

8. Intellectual Property Rights

Users should only upload content that they own or are legally authorized to share.

This includes:

  • lash photos;
  • creator images;
  • tutorials;
  • beauty references;
  • music;
  • videos;
  • logos;
  • salon materials.

Uploading copyrighted material without authorization may violate intellectual property laws and Kolly policies.

Examples of prohibited content include:

  • stolen creator content;
  • pirated media;
  • unauthorized reposts;
  • copyrighted music without permission;
  • copied courses or tutorials.

Repeated intellectual property violations may result in permanent suspension.

Kolly may comply with valid intellectual property takedown requests.

9. Spam, Manipulation, And Platform Abuse

Users may not manipulate platform systems or engagement metrics.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • fake accounts;
  • artificial likes or follows;
  • automated spam;
  • bots;
  • misleading metadata;
  • deceptive beauty claims;
  • mass unsolicited messaging;
  • engagement manipulation.

Users may not scrape, overload, reverse engineer, or interfere with Kolly systems or infrastructure.

10. Beauty And Eyelash Disclaimer

Kolly provides beauty inspiration, AI-assisted lash guidance, and informational beauty content.

The Service does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or healthcare services.

Beauty results may vary depending on:

  • eye sensitivity;
  • allergies;
  • lash condition;
  • adhesive quality;
  • salon practices;
  • stylist experience;
  • aftercare;
  • biological differences.

AI-generated beauty guidance is informational only and should not replace professional judgment.

Users should consult qualified professionals before making decisions involving cosmetic procedures or eye-area treatments.

11. Reporting And Moderation

Kolly may provide reporting, blocking, muting, and moderation tools.

Users are encouraged to report:

  • abusive conduct;
  • scams;
  • impersonation;
  • intellectual property violations;
  • unsafe beauty content;
  • explicit material;
  • harmful AI misuse.

Moderation actions may include:

  • warnings;
  • content removal;
  • reduced visibility;
  • AI feature restrictions;
  • account suspension;
  • permanent account termination.

Kolly may preserve moderation records, logs, device identifiers, and related evidence where necessary for safety, fraud prevention, platform integrity, or legal compliance.

12. Enforcement Rights

Kolly reserves the right to investigate potential violations of these guidelines and to take action at its sole discretion.

Enforcement decisions may depend on:

  • severity of the conduct;
  • repeated violations;
  • impact on users or platform safety;
  • legal obligations;
  • App Store compliance requirements.

Kolly may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these rules or create safety, operational, legal, or reputational risks.

13. Content Labeling

Users are expected to accurately label and describe content where applicable.

Misleading categories, deceptive tags, spam metadata, or intentionally false beauty descriptions are prohibited.

Users should avoid misleading claims regarding cosmetic results, certifications, or AI-generated recommendations.

14. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for all activities conducted through their accounts.

By using Kolly, you acknowledge that:

  • you use the Service at your own risk;
  • AI-generated outputs may contain inaccuracies;
  • beauty recommendations may not apply to every user;
  • you remain responsible for evaluating all beauty advice or creator content before relying on it.

Users are expected to behave respectfully and contribute positively to the Kolly community.
